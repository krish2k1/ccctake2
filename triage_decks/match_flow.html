12 / RULES OF THE ROAD

The three rules earned from prior rollouts

RULE 1

Don't take the decision.
Improve the decision.

The layer never says "use this vendor." It presents top-3 with rationale. The adjuster picks. Authority stays with the licensed claims professional.

RULE 2

Show your work.

Every factor visible. Every comparable shown. Every override pattern surfaced. Adjusters trust what they can audit — and so do regulators.

RULE 3

Make override frictionless.

Two clicks. Free-text allowed. Reason coded and trended. Not used for individual performance reviews in year one. Adoption beats accuracy in year one.

"A platform that makes adjusters feel surveilled will be sabotaged." — Dr. James Kim, panel transcript

13 / BEFORE / AFTER

An adjuster's morning — same caseload, two different worlds

BEFORE · today

Memory and habit

  • ⏱ 0:00 · open desk · 47 unread items
  • ⏱ 0:30 · triage the queue manually
  • ⏱ 1:15 · decide nurse is needed on three files · pick vendor from habit · email referral
  • ⏱ 2:30 · decide one file needs SIU · call the usual investigator · no comparison
  • ⏱ 3:45 · realize a 60-day-old file should have had nurse on day 7 · too late
  • Outcome: ~15 service decisions/day · zero comparison data · zero learning loop
AFTER · with the layer

Recommended defaults

  • ⏱ 0:00 · open desk · queue pre-sorted by recommended action
  • ⏱ 0:10 · review 8 pending recommendations · top-3 vendors visible · 6 accepted in <2 minutes
  • ⏱ 0:25 · 2 swaps with reason — local knowledge captured
  • ⏱ 0:30 · proactive alert: "this file is drifting · day 55 · litigation propensity rising · recommend D6 counsel"
  • ⏱ 0:35 · review surveillance approval queue — sign off after compliance review
  • Outcome: ~30 service decisions/day · all comparable · all auditable · all feeding the loop
