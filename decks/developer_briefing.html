// mission

What we are actually shipping

PRINCIPLE

Orchestration, not core replacement

The platform consumes events from claims/policy/billing and injects decisions/recommendations back. We do not own the system of record; we own the decision surface above it.

PRINCIPLE

Capability domains, bounded contexts

Seven capability domains, each owning its data, models, and APIs. Internal coordination via events. External coordination via well-defined contracts.

PRINCIPLE

ML lives inside the capability

Severity score lives in Intake. Litigation propensity lives in Litigation. Bill anomaly detection lives in Medical. There is no separate "AI module." The ML platform serves them; it does not host the business logic.

PRINCIPLE

Compliance is engineered in, not bolted on

Audit trail, explainability, and human-in-the-loop are cross-cutting NFRs from day one, not a phase-3 retrofit. Every model-influenced action is traceable end-to-end.

// domain 1 · intake & intelligence

Intake & Intelligence

What it does

  • Ingests FNOL from every channel into a canonical FNOL aggregate.
  • Runs document AI over medical records, FNOL artifacts, wage statements.
  • Produces an EarlySeverityScore within 24h of FNOL.
  • Emits FNOLIngested, DocumentParsed, SeverityScored.

Key models

  • severity-v1 — gradient-boosted; explainable; SHAP per prediction.
  • Document classifier + field extractor (buy, not build).

Contracts

POST /fnol GET /claim/{id}/severity GET /document/{id}/extraction

Consumes

claims-core::FNOLCreateddocuments::Uploaded

Produces

intake::FNOLIngestedintake::SeverityScoredintake::DocumentParsed

Owns data

FNOL canonical record · document extractions · severity score history · feature vectors

// domain 2 · triage & assignment

Triage & Assignment

What it does

  • Recommends desk routing based on severity, jurisdiction, complexity, current load.
  • Maintains dynamic caseload balance — complexity-weighted, not raw open-count.
  • Triggers nurse case management referral when duration/severity criteria cross threshold (target: by day 14 for qualifying claims).
  • Escalates files drifting from expected trajectory.

Key models

  • duration-v1 — expected RTW date prediction; drives drift detection.
  • NCM-referral classifier — should-have-nurse signal.

Contracts

GET /claim/{id}/routing POST /claim/{id}/reassign GET /adjuster/{id}/load

Consumes

intake::SeverityScoredclaims-core::ClaimUpdatedhr::AdjusterRosterChanged

Produces

triage::RoutingRecommendedtriage::NCMReferralTriggeredtriage::EscalationRaised

RULE  Routing recommendations are recommendations; the adjuster's supervisor confirms reassignments touching customer continuity.

// domain 3 · medical management

Medical Management

What it does

  • Real-time anomaly detection on medical bills (upcoding, unbundling, OON).
  • Provider outcome scoring: cost, duration, RTW success rate.
  • Network steering recommendations consistent with MPN/PPO rules.
  • NCM workflow: task assignment, documentation, outcome capture.

Key models

  • bill-anomaly-v1 — anomaly detection over bill features + CPT history.
  • provider-score-v1 — outcome-adjusted ranking with case-mix controls.

Contracts

POST /bill/review GET /provider/{npi}/score POST /claim/{id}/ncm-task

Consumes

vendor::BillReceivedmedical::TreatmentRecorded

Produces

medical::BillAnomalyDetectedmedical::ProviderScoredmedical::SteeringSuggested

RULE  No auto-denial of treatment. Anomalies flow to adjuster review with rationale.

// domain 4 · litigation management

Litigation Management

What it does

  • Litigation propensity score within 30 days of FNOL.
  • Early settlement workflow for high-propensity files meeting criteria.
  • Defense counsel selection + rate card application.
  • Settlement reserve range from historical comparables, case-mix adjusted.

Key models

  • litigation-propensity-v1 — P(attorney represented) from claim features + adjuster note NLP.
  • settlement-range-v1 — nearest-neighbor with calibration.

// domain 5 · vendor orchestration

Vendor Orchestration

What it does

  • Single canonical vendor master, deduplicated across claims/finance.
  • Rate-card enforcement — every invoice validated against contracted rates.
  • Vendor performance scorecard: cost, quality, SLA, claim outcome impact.
  • Anomaly detection on DME, transportation, translation, surveillance billing.

Why it matters

15–20% of LAE waste sits in vendor leakage. This domain is the single highest-ROI rules engine in the platform.

Contracts

Consumes

Produces

BUILD  No good off-the-shelf product covers WC vendor orchestration end-to-end. This is build.

// domain 6 · reserve & reporting

Reserve & Reporting

What it does

  • Actuarial extracts — severity-adjusted IBNR inputs, development triangles, segmentations.
  • Statutory expense allocation aligned to IEE Part III.
  • Operational dashboards: LAE by category, state, cohort, adjuster, vendor.
  • Executive scorecard tied to business objectives.

Models

  • No black-box reserve models. Explainability required — actuarial + DOI.
  • Models are inputs to reserving, never the reserve itself.

Produces

// domain 7 · data & ML platform

Data & ML Platform

What it does

  • Event-driven ingestion from claims/policy/billing/vendors/documents.
  • Canonical claims-centric data model — policy → claim → medical bill → vendor invoice linked.
  • Feature store powering all production ML.
  • Model registry with version, lineage, performance, owner, approvals.
  • Production monitoring: prediction drift, data drift, fairness metrics.

Foundational principles

  • One canonical claim ID across systems.
  • Idempotent processing throughout.
  • PII minimization in non-prod — synthetic/masked data only.

Capabilities surfaced

feature-store model-registry batch-scoring online-scoring drift-monitor decision-log

BUILD
Decision orchestration, feature store, model registry surface, vendor performance layer, claim-360 canonical model
// non-functional requirements · cross-cutting

What must be true everywhere

NF-COMP-01
Full audit trail for every AI-influenced decision: model version, inputs, outputs, human overrides, final action. Queryable, retained per regulatory minimums.
NF-COMP-02
Bias testing pre-deployment and at-least-annually for every production model influencing claim outcomes. Documented; available to regulators.
NF-COMP-03
Human review required for compensability decisions, payment denials, settlements above defined threshold. Enforced by the rules engine — not optional.
NF-COMP-04
Jurisdiction-aware rules engine — every state's nuances explicit. Zero single-state-assumption logic anywhere in the codebase.
NF-COMP-06
Prompt-pay statutes encoded; payment timelines monitored; violations alarmed.
NF-SEC-01
HIPAA/HITECH-aligned controls for medical data: encryption at rest + in transit, full access logging, role-based access.
NF-ARCH-02
Idempotent event handlers. Events will be replayed. Downstream actions must be safe to repeat.
NF-OPS-01
RPO ≤ 1h · RTO ≤ 4h for claims processing capability. 99.5% uptime for adjuster-facing surfaces during business hours.
NF-EXP-01
Per-prediction explainability for any model influencing a money decision. SHAP or equivalent, surfaced in adjuster UI.

// build sequence

What ships when

Phase 1 · Foundation M0–M9

  • Claims-360 data platform (event ingestion + canonical model)
  • Document AI for intake
  • Severity scoring model v1
  • Vendor performance scorecard (rules-driven)
  • Decision-audit-trail framework
→ 0.4–0.6 LAE pts

Phase 2 · Optimization M9–M24

  • Medical-only triage automation (conservative STP)
  • Real-time medical bill anomaly detection
  • Litigation propensity model + early settlement workflow
  • RTW orchestration
  • Dynamic caseload balancing
→ 1.0–1.2 LAE pts

Phase 3 · Closed Loop M24–M36

  • Provider outcome scoring → network steering
  • Vendor billing fraud detection
  • Adjuster knowledge assistant (GenAI)
  • Continuous model-retraining infrastructure
→ 0.6–0.7 LAE pts

Sequencing logic: Phase 1 lays the data + audit foundation that everything else depends on. Phase 2 introduces models against real outcome data. Phase 3 closes the loop with continuous retraining.

// definition of done · phase 1

What Phase 1 must demonstrate before Phase 2 starts

  • Canonical claims-360 record exists for at least 80% of active claims, linking policy → claim → medical bill → vendor invoice.
  • Document AI in production for at least three document types (medical records, wage statements, FNOL attachments) with measured extraction accuracy.
  • Severity score v1 running on every new FNOL with documented model card, bias-testing report, and SHAP explainability surfaced in the adjuster UI.
  • Vendor scorecard live across at least the top 80% of vendor spend, with rate cards loaded and invoice validation flagging exceptions.
  • Decision audit trail capturing every AI-influenced decision; queryable by claim, adjuster, model, time range.
  • Operating-model change evidenced: at least one cohort of adjusters working the new triage flow with measured time-on-task improvement.
  • LAE impact measurable: instrumented attribution against a control cohort; on track to capture 0.4–0.6 LAE points within 12 months of Phase 1 completion.
