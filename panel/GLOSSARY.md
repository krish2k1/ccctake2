# Glossary
## Plain-language definitions for terms used in the panel

| Term | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **WC** | Workers' compensation insurance — covers workplace injury (medical + lost wages) | The line of business this platform serves |
| **DWP** | Direct Written Premium — total premium written by the carrier | Anchors carrier size; mid-size = $300M–$1B |
| **Expense Ratio** | Underwriting expense ÷ premium (definitions vary — statutory vs GAAP) | One of two halves of the combined ratio |
| **LAE** | Loss Adjustment Expense — what it costs to handle claims | The primary lever for this program |
| **ALAE** | Allocated LAE — expenses attributable to a specific claim (defense, IME, surveillance) | Reducible via litigation management + vendor discipline |
| **ULAE** | Unallocated LAE — claims department overhead (salaries, systems) | Reducible via automation + caseload management |
| **FNOL** | First Notice of Loss — when a claim is reported | Earliest data point; biggest triage opportunity |
| **FROI / SROI** | First Report of Injury / Subsequent Report of Injury — state-mandated EDI reporting | Compliance must-haves; vary by state |
| **IBNR** | Incurred But Not Reported — actuarial reserve for claims that have happened but aren't on the books yet | Better data tightens the estimate; reduces capital needs |
| **RTW** | Return-to-Work — getting an injured worker back on the job (often modified duty) | Every day in RTW reduces indemnity, medical, AND LAE |
| **NCM** | Nurse Case Manager — RN who guides medical treatment plan | Early NCM engagement is one of the highest-ROI LAE levers |
| **MPN / PPO** | Medical Provider Network / Preferred Provider Organization — contracted provider list | Steering claimants to high-quality, in-network providers reduces costs |
| **STP** | Straight-Through Processing — claim handled with minimal human intervention | Achievable for clean med-only; not for lost-time |
| **NCCI** | National Council on Compensation Insurance — loss costs, data standards | Statistical and rate-filing authority for most states |
| **NAIC** | National Association of Insurance Commissioners — state regulator coordination body | Source of the AI Model Bulletin |
| **DOI** | Department of Insurance — the state-level regulator | They enforce; market conduct exams are real |
| **HIPAA** | Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act | Medical records handling rules; WC interaction is nuanced |
| **MMSEA / CMS Section 111** | Medicare Mandatory Reporting for liability/WC claims involving Medicare beneficiaries | Federal reporting; penalties for non-compliance |
| **UCSPA** | Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Act — state-by-state | Sets behavior standards for handling claims |
| **IEE** | Insurance Expense Exhibit — statutory expense allocation schedule | The way regulators see expense |
| **EDI** | Electronic Data Interchange — structured B2B messaging | How carriers exchange FROI/SROI with states |
| **IME** | Independent Medical Examination — second opinion exam | An ALAE line item |
| **SIU** | Special Investigation Unit — fraud investigation | An ALAE line item |
