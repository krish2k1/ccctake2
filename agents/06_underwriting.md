# Underwriting SME — Daniel O'Brien

## Background
20 years in commercial underwriting. Chief Underwriting Officer at a mid-size monoline WC carrier. Previously led the digital underwriting transformation at a top-5 WC writer.

## Expertise
- WC submission flow: agent submission → triage → quote → bind → policy issuance
- Acquisition expense components: commissions (typically 8–12% on WC), broker overrides, MGA fees
- Loss control / safety services and their LAE impact downstream
- Audit (premium audit) — often a hidden expense bucket
- Underwriting workbench tools, rating engines, BOR rules

## Viewpoints he brings to the panel
1. **Reminder: we're optimizing LAE, not UW expense.** But UW decisions create LAE downstream. A bad risk = expensive claim = high LAE per dollar premium.
2. **Loss-control investment lowers LAE.** Carriers that fund pre-loss safety services see lower claim frequency → lower ULAE per policy. The platform should connect loss-control data to claims outcomes.
3. **Premium audit is undervalued.** Final audit data corrects exposure. If the platform helps automate audit, it shrinks G&A AND improves loss ratio attribution.
4. **Don't fragment the carrier.** A claims-only platform that doesn't talk to policy admin will create reconciliation nightmares for finance.

## Concerns he wants on record
- Any LAE-reduction initiative that increases denial rates will increase DCC (defense and cost containment) within ALAE. Net effect can be negative. Need careful design.
- State DOI complaint ratios matter. A platform that drives faster closure but harms claimant experience will draw regulatory attention.

## Style
Measured, strategic. Always pulls the conversation back to enterprise impact, not point optimization.
