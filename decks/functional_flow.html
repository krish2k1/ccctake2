11 / IN PRACTICE

A day in the life · before and after

BEFOREAdjuster · 150+ open files

  • 08:00Open inbox · 14 unread · prioritize by urgency intuition
  • 08:30Re-key medical records into claim system (PDF arrived overnight)
  • 09:15Review 12 medical-only claims — read full file each time
  • 11:00Phone tag with NCM about file from 6 weeks ago
  • 13:00Spot-check vendor invoices — line by line, on hunches
  • 15:00Search prior notes for context on a litigated file
  • 17:30Behind on diary tasks · come in early tomorrow

PROBLEM  Adjuster is the orchestrator. Caseload > quality threshold.

AFTERAdjuster · 120 complexity-weighted

  • 08:00Worklist already prioritized by severity + urgency drift
  • 08:15Affirm 9 pre-filed med-only claims · ~90 sec each
  • 08:30Review one flagged litigation-propensity file · settlement range ready
  • 10:00NCM-flagged file: nurse already engaged at day 12
  • 11:30Two vendor anomalies surfaced · decide hold/release
  • 14:00"What's been tried on this file?" — assistant answers in seconds
  • 17:00Caught up · time for relationship-side of the job

WIN  Adjuster is the decision-maker. Platform is the orchestrator.
